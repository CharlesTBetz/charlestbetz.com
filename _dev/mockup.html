Project

Five Minutes On The Low Road

A woman has a near-death experience, and lives with the consequences. A new musical in development — book by Josiah Thomas Turner, music & lyrics by Charles T. Betz.

We're working from research at the International Association for Near-Death Studies and field interviews with experiencers. Josiah joined the project in February 2025 and is shaping the book with tremendous insight.
