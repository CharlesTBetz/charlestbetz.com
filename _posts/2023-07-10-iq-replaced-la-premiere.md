*IQ: The Musical* had its world premiere on **July 10–11, 2023** at **The Broadwater Main Stage** in Los Angeles, as part of [New Musicals Inc.](http://nmi.org)'s evening program **"Replaced! — Six Short Musicals About Obsolescence."**

The 15-minute piece was the culmination of a full year in NMI's Core program — concept through staged performance, with professional directors, music directors, and performers bringing the work to life. Full performance video above.
