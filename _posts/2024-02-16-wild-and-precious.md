## The Text

We are wild and brief and precious  
In the eyes of those we love  
We are droplets in the rainstorm flying high  
We are petals from a blossom  
Of an ancient cherry tree  
Soaring on the wind across the sky

We are wild and brief and precious  
In the stillness of the moon  
We are sparks against the sunset of the years  
We are silk and sable feathers  
From the wise old raven's tail  
Landing on the mountain wet with tears

Oh hear me  
Hear my voice today  
Hear me like a poet hears a rhyme

Call me  
Call my name out loud  
Call me as I gently fade from time

We are wild and brief and precious  
In the presence of the Lord  
Smoke and ashes fading in the sun  
We are pilgrims walking onwards  
Through a land of silver dreams  
Many rivers flowing into one

We are wild and brief and precious  
In the presence of the Lord
