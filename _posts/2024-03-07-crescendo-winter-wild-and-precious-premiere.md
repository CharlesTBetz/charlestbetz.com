The **[Crescendo Community Chorus](https://www.crescendochorus.org/)** Winter Concert, **March 7, 2024** at **Plymouth Congregational Church** ([1919 LaSalle Ave, Minneapolis](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QSv3gtsU9Rdp3WzGA)), 7:30 PM. Free admission.

Program included the **world premiere of the commissioned choral/orchestra arrangement of "Wild and Precious"** — and I also assisted with transcribing the Sibelius choral work *"This Is My Song"* for orchestra.

Dr. Randal Buikema conducted; Franco Holder accompanied; Aimee Bryant guest soloist; with members of the Gilbert & Sullivan Opera Company orchestra.
