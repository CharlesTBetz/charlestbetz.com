Just was informed that First Presbyterian Church in Evanston will be performing [Wild and Precious](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOvH8a_IizI) in honor of Kay Sugiura, who sung in their choir for many years. Their music director said, "Your work will minister deeply not only to our choir, but to our congregation, and we're so grateful for the opportunity to share it in memory of Kay." This is a nice vote of confidence in the piece. They performed the piece on Nov 3 @ 9:25 am (CST) - it streamed to [firstpresevanston.org](http://firstpresevanston.org/).

In other news, I have nearly completed the music for the first act of Five Minutes on the Low Road. Due to very badly timed Covid I was unable to attend the IANDS conference in Phoenix, which was disappointing but has not delayed my progress.
