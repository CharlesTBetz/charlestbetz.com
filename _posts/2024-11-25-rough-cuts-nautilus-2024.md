**Nautilus Music-Theater** *Rough Cuts!* — selections from the 2024 Composer-Librettist Studio. Two nights, two venues:

- **Monday, November 25, 7:30 PM** — at the Nautilus Music-Theater studio, first floor of the Northern Warehouse, **308 Prince Street**, Lowertown St. Paul.
- **Tuesday, November 26, 7:30 PM** — at **Walker Community Church**, 3104 16th Avenue (at 31st St, near Bloomington and Lake), Minneapolis.

Local writers Angela Frucci, Al Justiniano, Blossom Johnson, Brian James Polak, and **Josiah Thomas Turner** were joined by composers Charles Betz, Peter Durow, Tim Kirchhof, Ken Takata, and Carter-Quinn Tanis — new pieces written for, and performed by, Julia Isabel Diaz, Xan Mattek, Keri Rodau, Joshua Row, and Justin Spenner, under the guidance of **Ben Krywosz** and **Sonja Thompson**, assisted by Eric Broker. Sketches and songs that offer a fascinating glimpse into the collaborative process of making new opera and music-theater.

This was the culminating showcase of the [Nautilus Composer-Librettist Studio](https://nautilus-genome-project.squarespace.com/2024-mn-cl-studio) intensive that ran November 9–24 — and the start of what would become *Five Minutes On The Low Road* with Josiah Thomas Turner. (Two of those songs would surface again at [Rough Cuts in March 2026](/blog/2026/03/rough-cuts-nautilus/).)
