Big news for Five Minutes On The Low Road! [Josiah Thomas Turner](https://newplayexchange.org/users/20167/josiah-thomas-turner), who I met at the Nautilus Composer-Librettist Studio, is taking over book writing! As musical theater pros know, book is hard and being a "triple threat" (doing book, lyrics, and music) is insane. As the legendary [Lehmann Engel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_Engel), founder of Broadway Musicals, Inc., reportedly said, "Sure, you're free to do all three. You're also free to commit suicide." I am very glad to have a partner on this project.
