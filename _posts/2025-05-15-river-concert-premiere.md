The **[Crescendo Community Chorus](https://www.crescendochorus.org/)** Spring Concert — *A Celebration of the Mississippi River*, in partnership with **Friends of the Mississippi River**. **May 15, 2025** at **DeLaSalle High School** (1 DeLaSalle Drive, Minneapolis, on Nicollet Island), 7:30 PM. Free admission. Conducted by Randal A. Buikema, accompanied by Franco Holder, with Heid Erdrich (Minneapolis Poet Laureate 2024).

The program included the **world premiere of "River Song."** The livestream link has since expired, but you can watch the video-with-imagery version above, or the [score video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4UDeDlYxI8) performed by the Macrófona choir of Argentina.
