Two songs from *Five Minutes On The Low Road* were performed at Nautilus Music-Theater's **Rough Cuts** program in St. Paul — and nearly **50 people** showed up despite a March blizzard. This is Minnesota. We don't cancel for snow.

Soprano **Norah Long** performed **"Best for Her"** and **"This Side of Tomorrow,"** with Josh Row (tenor) and Jake Endres (baritone). Josiah Thomas Turner (librettist) and I were coaching the performers in real time during Monday's open rehearsal — a rare window into how new musical theater gets built. By Tuesday's show, the pieces had been shaped and sharpened.

![Charlie Betz (composer), Josiah Thomas Turner (librettist), Josh Row (tenor), Jake Endres (baritone), Norah Long (soprano)]({{ "/assets/images/shows/rough-cuts-2026-03.jpg" | relative_url }})
*Charlie Betz (composer), Josiah Thomas Turner (librettist), Josh Row (tenor), Jake Endres (baritone), Norah Long (soprano)*

The consensus among everyone involved: one of the best Rough Cuts in memory. Musical direction by Sonja Thompson.
