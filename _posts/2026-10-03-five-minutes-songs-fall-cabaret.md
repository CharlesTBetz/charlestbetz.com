The New Musical Theater Exchange stages its **Fall Cabaret** at Crooners on **Monday, October 12**, and two of the songs on the program are mine, from *Five Minutes On The Low Road*: **<a href="https://5minlowroad.com/listen/#this-side-of-tomorrow" target="_blank" rel="noopener">"This Side of Tomorrow"</a>** and **<a href="https://5minlowroad.com/listen/#earth-was-the-dream" target="_blank" rel="noopener">"Earth Was the Dream."</a>** They'll be sung by **<a href="https://www.jenburleigh-bentz.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Jen Burleigh-Bentz</a>** and **<a href="https://www.jakeendres.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Jake Endres</a>**.

![NMTE's 2026 Fall Cabaret at Crooners]({{ "/assets/images/shows/fall-cabaret-2026.jpg" | relative_url }})

The show runs **6:00–7:30 PM**, and doors open at **5:00 PM** for dinner and drinks. Tickets are **$25–$35**, all ages are welcome, and parking is free.

**<a href="https://www.croonersmn.com/event/new-musical-theatre-exchanges-2026-fall-cabaret/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Get tickets at Crooners</a>** · <a href="https://nmte.org/events/2026/10/12/fall-cabaret-crooners/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Full lineup on the NMTE site</a>

Hope to see you there.
